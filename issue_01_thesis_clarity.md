# Issue: Strengthen Thesis Statement Clarity

**Labels:** academic-writing, thesis

## Context
Clear thesis statements are the backbone of any academic paper. They should be specific, arguable, and located early in the document.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Thesis is stated in the first two paragraphs
- [ ] Thesis is a single, clear sentence
- [ ] Thesis is arguable (not just a fact statement)
- [ ] All body paragraphs connect directly to the thesis

Reference: Genskow & Shapiro style guide - Thesis Structure section
