# Issue: Evidence Integration Standards

**Labels:** academic-writing, evidence

## Context
Claims must be supported by evidence. Weak evidence integration undermines the argument's credibility.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Every major claim has at least one supporting citation
- [ ] Evidence is introduced with signal phrases (e.g., "According to...", "Smith (2020) argues...")
- [ ] Evidence is explained, not just quoted
- [ ] Counter-evidence is acknowledged and addressed

Reference: Genskow & Shapiro style guide - Evidence-Based Arguments section
