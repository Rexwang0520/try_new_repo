# Issue: Eliminate Jargon and Hedging

**Labels:** academic-writing, clarity

## Context
Overly complex language obscures meaning. Clear, direct prose is preferred in economics writing.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] No jargon without definition
- [ ] Hedging language (e.g., "it is believed that") replaced with direct claims
- [ ] Sentences average under 30 words
- [ ] Passive voice minimized (<10% of sentences)

## Notes
Reference: Genskow & Shapiro style guide - Concise Writing section
