# Issue: Peer Review and Revision Process

**Labels:** academic-writing, revision

## Context
Quality academic writing requires multiple revision cycles. A structured revision process improves clarity and rigor.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Draft 1: Focus on structure and argument flow
- [ ] Draft 2: Focus on clarity and concision
- [ ] Draft 3: Focus on citation and formatting
- [ ] External reader review completed before final submission
- [ ] Revision log or tracked changes maintained
