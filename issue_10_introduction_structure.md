# Issue: Introduction Section Structure

**Labels:** academic-writing, introduction

## Context
The introduction sets up the problem, establishes relevance, and presents the thesis. It should hook the reader and provide roadmap.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Opens with a hook (question, statistic, or compelling observation)
- [ ] Establishes the economic relevance of the topic
- [ ] Reviews existing literature briefly (what do we know?)
- [ ] Identifies the gap (what don't we know?)
- [ ] States the thesis/objective clearly
- [ ] Provides a roadmap of the paper structure
